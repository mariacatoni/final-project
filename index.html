My Life Through Concerts

Each dot is one concert date (data exported from Concert Data.xlsx). If you saw multiple shows on the same day, they share a dot. The timeline is one continuous horizontal strip from 2000 to 2025, with section labels for 2000–2009, 2010–2019, and 2020–2025. Each column is labeled with the full year (for example, 2007). Hover a dot to see artists from column B (comma-separated).
